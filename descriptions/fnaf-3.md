# Five Nights at Freddy's 3

## Overview

Five Nights at Freddy's 3 takes place decades later at Fazbear's Fright, a horror attraction built from salvaged animatronic parts. A single animatronic — Springtrap — hunts you through a maze of cameras, vents, and malfunctioning systems.

This browser version is a **fan-made HTML5 port** from the [hd_fnaf](https://github.com/irv77/hd_fnaf) project. It is **not** an official Scott Cawthon release and is not approved by or associated with the original creator.

## Game Information

**Genre:** Horror / Survival

**Developer:** Fan port (original game by Scott Cawthon)

## Features

- Springtrap as the primary threat
- Vent and camera surveillance network
- Audio lure system to redirect the animatronic
- Reboot panels to fix failing systems
- Runs in the browser with no install

## How to Play

Monitor cameras and vents to track Springtrap's position. Use audio lures to draw him away from your office. Reboot failing camera, vent, or audio systems before they leave you blind. Survive five nights at the haunted attraction.

## Controls

- Mouse to interact with cameras, vents, audio, and reboot panels
- Press `-=` for fullscreen (per hd_fnaf)

## Platform Support

Runs in desktop browsers when served locally via the game manager or a static HTTP server. Self-contained with relative asset paths.

## Why Play

A leaner, atmosphere-heavy entry focused on one relentless hunter — Springtrap's unpredictable movement through vents makes every night a tense cat-and-mouse game.
