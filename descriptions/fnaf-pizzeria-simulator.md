# Five Nights at Freddy's: Pizzeria Simulator

## Overview

Five Nights at Freddy's: Pizzeria Simulator (also known as Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria Simulator) blends pizzeria management with survival horror. Run your restaurant by day — buying supplies, placing items, and attracting customers — then survive animatronic attacks in the office by night.

This browser version is a **fan-made HTML5 port** from the [hd_fnaf](https://github.com/irv77/hd_fnaf) project. It is **not** an official Scott Cawthon release and is not approved by or associated with the original creator.

## Game Information

**Genre:** Horror / Simulation

**Developer:** Fan port (original game by Scott Cawthon)

## Features

- Daytime pizzeria management and customization
- Nighttime office survival with ventilation and audio systems
- Salvaging animatronics for parts and revenue
- Multiple endings tied to your business decisions
- Runs in the browser with no install

## How to Play

During the day, use your budget to buy decor, menu items, and animatronics to build your pizzeria. At night, monitor vents, adjust temperature, play audio cues, and keep hostile animatronics out of your office. Balance profit with survival to progress the story.

## Controls

- Mouse to interact with management UI, vents, and office controls
- Press `-=` for fullscreen (per hd_fnaf)

## Platform Support

Runs in desktop browsers when served locally via the game manager or a static HTTP server. Self-contained with relative asset paths.

## Why Play

A clever mashup of tycoon sim and classic FNAF tension — your daytime choices directly affect how dangerous each night becomes.
