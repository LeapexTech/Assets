# Five Nights at Freddy's: Ultimate Custom Night

## Overview

Five Nights at Freddy's: Ultimate Custom Night is a customizable survival challenge featuring dozens of animatronics from across the FNAF series. Set each character's AI difficulty on a 0–20 scale, pick your office layout, and try to survive until 6 AM.

This browser version is a **fan-made HTML5 port** from the [hd_fnaf](https://github.com/irv77/hd_fnaf) project. It is **not** an official Scott Cawthon release and is not approved by or associated with the original creator.

## Game Information

**Genre:** Horror / Survival

**Developer:** Fan port (original game by Scott Cawthon)

## Features

- 50+ selectable animatronics from the entire series
- Per-character AI difficulty from 0 to 20
- Multiple office layouts and challenge presets
- High-score and completionist challenge modes
- Runs in the browser with no install

## How to Play

Select animatronics and set their difficulty levels before starting a night. Use every tool from across the series — doors, mask, flashlight, heater, audio lures, and more — depending on which characters are active. Survive until 6 AM or beat community challenge presets.

## Controls

- Mouse to interact with selection menus and in-game controls
- Press `-=` for fullscreen (per hd_fnaf)

## Platform Support

Runs in desktop browsers when served locally via the game manager or a static HTTP server. Self-contained with relative asset paths.

## Why Play

The ultimate FNAF gauntlet — crank every animatronic to max difficulty for a brutal test, or craft your own nightmare roster from the entire franchise.
