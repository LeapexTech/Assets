# Five Nights at Freddy's 2

## Overview

Five Nights at Freddy's 2 is a prequel set at a revamped Freddy Fazbear's Pizza with a new cast of animatronics. There are no protective doors this time — instead you wear a Freddy mask, wind a music box, and use flashlight checks to survive the night.

This browser version is a **fan-made HTML5 port** from the [hd_fnaf](https://github.com/irv77/hd_fnaf) project. It is **not** an official Scott Cawthon release and is not approved by or associated with the original creator.

## Game Information

**Genre:** Horror / Survival

**Developer:** Fan port (original game by Scott Cawthon)

## Features

- New animatronics including Toy Freddy, Mangle, and Balloon Boy
- Freddy mask disguise mechanic
- Music box winding to keep The Puppet at bay
- Flashlight checks down the hallway
- Runs in the browser with no install

## How to Play

Survive five (plus bonus) nights without doors to lock. Put on the Freddy mask when animatronics enter your office. Keep the music box wound via the cameras. Flash the hallway to spot Foxy and other threats before they reach you.

## Controls

- Mouse to interact with cameras, mask, flashlight, and music box
- Press `-=` for fullscreen (per hd_fnaf)

## Platform Support

Runs in desktop browsers when served locally via the game manager or a static HTTP server. Self-contained with relative asset paths.

## Why Play

FNAF 2 cranks up the paranoia with faster animatronics, new mechanics, and some of the series' most memorable characters — a must-play sequel in the browser.
