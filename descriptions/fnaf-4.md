# Five Nights at Freddy's 4

## Overview

Five Nights at Freddy's 4 shifts the horror to a child's bedroom. Nightmare animatronics creep from the closet and hall doors while you listen for breathing, check blind spots with a flashlight, and hold the doors shut.

This browser version is a **fan-made HTML5 port** from the [hd_fnaf](https://github.com/irv77/hd_fnaf) project. It is **not** an official Scott Cawthon release and is not approved by or associated with the original creator.

## Game Information

**Genre:** Horror / Survival

**Developer:** Fan port (original game by Scott Cawthon)

## Features

- Bedroom setting with closet, doors, and bed threats
- Nightmare versions of classic animatronics
- Audio-based detection — listen for breathing
- Flashlight and door mechanics
- Runs in the browser with no install

## How to Play

Survive until 6 AM from your bed. Shine the flashlight down hall doors and into the closet to spot Nightmare animatronics. Hold doors shut when you hear breathing. Watch the bed behind you for smaller threats. React fast — mistakes are punished immediately.

## Controls

- Mouse to interact with flashlight, doors, and closet
- Press `-=` for fullscreen (per hd_fnaf)

## Platform Support

Runs in desktop browsers when served locally via the game manager or a static HTTP server. Self-contained with relative asset paths.

## Why Play

The most personal FNAF entry trades security cameras for claustrophobic bedroom terror — every creak and breath keeps you on edge until dawn.
