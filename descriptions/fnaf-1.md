# Five Nights at Freddy's

## Overview

Five Nights at Freddy's is the original survival horror game where you take a night-shift security job at Freddy Fazbear's Pizza. Monitor security cameras, conserve limited power, and keep the animatronic mascots out of your office until 6 AM.

This browser version is a **fan-made HTML5 port** from the [hd_fnaf](https://github.com/irv77/hd_fnaf) project. It is **not** an official Scott Cawthon release and is not approved by or associated with the original creator.

## Game Information

**Genre:** Horror / Survival

**Developer:** Fan port (original game by Scott Cawthon)

## Features

- Camera surveillance across the pizzeria
- Limited power management for doors and lights
- Unpredictable animatronic AI behavior
- Jump-scare tension across five nights
- Runs in the browser with no install

## How to Play

Survive from midnight to 6 AM each night. Watch the cameras to track Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, and Foxy. Close the security doors and toggle hallway lights only when needed — power drains quickly. Listen for movement cues in the dark.

## Controls

- Mouse to interact with cameras, doors, and lights
- Press `-=` for fullscreen (per hd_fnaf)

## Platform Support

Runs in desktop browsers when served locally via the game manager or a static HTTP server. Self-contained with relative asset paths.

## Why Play

The game that started the FNAF phenomenon — tense resource management, iconic animatronics, and jump scares that defined a generation of horror gaming, playable right in your browser.
